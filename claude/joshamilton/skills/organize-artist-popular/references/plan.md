# The plan

Step 4 of `SKILL.md`: what the plan holds.

## 1. The plan

Write the plan as a Markdown file and give it to the user. It has these sections, in order:

- A summary: what the folder holds now and what it will hold following the move.

- The proposed final tree.

- A table of every current folder: current path, decision (keep, rename, split, Delete), and the reason (for Delete, which rule).

- CUE `FILE` line fixes needed before the split in step 6.

- Single-file rips to split in step 6, and soundtrack albums sent to Delete (list them under "flagged for manual review").

- Missing releases: Studio, Live, and Compilation, with year.

- Questions, each with your recommended answer. Ask about anything the rules do not settle. Keep each question short and state the concrete choice.

- After the user answers, rewrite the plan with the answers in place. Delete the "Questions" section. Do not create a "Decisions" section. Make the moves once they approve. Albums the user keeps but wants to look at again go in a "flagged for manual review" list, in the plan and in your final message.
